# Legal and Transparency Notes

Not legal advice. External legal/compliance review remains required.

Current primary-source checks on 2026-08-26:

- German UWG §7: B2B telephone advertising to another market participant requires at least presumed consent; the model must not decide this on its own.
- German StGB §201: unauthorized recording of non-public spoken words is legally sensitive; recording is therefore OFF in this build unless separately approved.
- EU AI Act Article 50: AI systems interacting directly with natural persons are subject to transparency obligations; this package explicitly identifies itself as an AI call agent at first interaction.
